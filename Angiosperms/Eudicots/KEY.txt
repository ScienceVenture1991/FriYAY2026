Question about their flower?

  Asterales         Looks like one flower, is actually a lot of tiny flowers
  Caryophyllales    Frilly edges
  Lamiales          Strong scent, lots of small flowers in a spike or cluster
  Malpighiales      Dark markings on the petals
  Malvales          Big, trumpet-shaped flowers
  Proteales         Grow in muddy water, but the flower pokes from the surface
  Ranunculales      Thin, papery petals
  Rosales           5 petals in the wild, cousins with apples and strawberries
  Saxifragales      Petals are soft-textured and have a ruffled look about them
