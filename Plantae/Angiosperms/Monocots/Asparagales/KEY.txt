ASPARAGALES - Named after asparagus, of all things. Orchids, onions, aloe, vanilla, and saffron all live in this order!

================

  Amaryllidaceae    A cup or "tube" sticks out of the middle of the petals
  Iridaceae         Three petals stand up, three flop down
  Orchidaceae       One petal is a big fancy lip
