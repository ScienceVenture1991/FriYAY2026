MISSION 2: MOVE YOUR SPECIMEN

1. Run dir to see your flower's filename. Then send a copy of it
   to the Herbarium:

	copy yourflower.txt ..\..\..\..\..\Herbarium

   Each ..\ means "one folder up." You're five folders below
   Plantae, so you'll need five in total

2. Climb back up to Plantae:
     cd ..      (five times)

3. Go into the Herbarium and check your file arrived:
     cd Herbarium
     dir

4. Make a folder with your name (no spaces) and move your file in:
     mkdir YourName
     move yourflower.txt YourName

5. Read your next mission:
     type NOTICE.txt
